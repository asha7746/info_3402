3. Top 10 Products by Sales Revenue

Business Question

What are our top selling products by sales revenue?

Analysis

This chart compares the ten products that generated the most sales revenue. Unlike the previous chart which focused more on the number of units sold, this chart focuses on the total money earned from each product.

The Adventure Works Desktop PC2.33 XD233 Silver generated the most revenue at approximately $99,000. Several other desktop computers and televisions were also with the highest earning products.

This shows that a product does not need to sell the most units in order to generate the most revenue.
