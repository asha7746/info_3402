Project Overview

This project uses Power BI to analyze sales data from 2021 to 2024. The goal is to understand product sales, store performance, customer shopping habits, and overall profits.

I created nine visualizations to answer different business questions. These include top selling products, customer spending, delivery times, and changes in sales and profit.

1. Star Schema

Before creating my charts, I connected four tables in Power BI using a Star Schema. This helps organize the data and allows information from different tables to work together seamlessly.

Tables Used

factSales: Contains the sales transactions, including quantities, prices, dates, and customer, product, and store keys.

dimProduct: Contains information about each product, including its name, brand, and category.

dimCustomer: Contains customer information such as age, gender, name, and location.

dimStore: Contains information about store locations, including store names, countries, and states.

The factSales table connects to the three dimension tables using their matching keys. This allows Power BI to compare sales across products, customers, and stores.
