10. Spending by Gender and Country

Business Question

Which genders spend the most, and where do they live?

Analysis

This chart compares spending between male and female customers across different countries. Each color represents a country, making it easier to see where customers are located.

Male customers spent a bit more overall than female customers. The United States accounted for the largest amount of spending for both genders.

The United Kingdom, Germany, and Canada also contributed a lot of spending. The chart shows that customer spending is spread across a lot of countries but the United States makes up the largest share of spending.
